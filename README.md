# SQL Business Case Portfolio

A collection of **10 real-world SQL business cases** designed to demonstrate practical SQL skills and business problem-solving abilities for Data Analyst roles.

Each case represents a common analytical task encountered in retail and business environments, covering customer behavior, sales performance, inventory management, supplier evaluation, and executive reporting.

---

# Business Cases

## 01. Customer Retention Analysis

### Business Question

Which customers meet the company's loyalty criteria based on purchasing frequency, revenue contribution, and return history?

### Business Insight

Identify high-value customers for loyalty programs, personalized marketing campaigns, and long-term customer retention strategies.

---

## 02. Top Product by Category

### Business Question

Which product generates the highest revenue within each product category?

### Business Insight

Highlight best-selling products across categories to support merchandising, inventory planning, and promotional decisions.

---

## 03. Sales Performance Analysis

### Business Question

Which products, categories, and periods contribute the most to overall sales performance?

### Business Insight

Evaluate sales performance to identify growth opportunities and optimize business strategy.

---

## 04. Promotion Performance Analysis

### Business Question

Which promotions generate the highest sales while maintaining healthy profit margins?

### Business Insight

Measure promotional effectiveness to improve future marketing investments and campaign performance.

---

## 05. Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) Ranking

### Business Question

Which customers generate the highest lifetime value for the business?

### Business Insight

Prioritize high-value customers for retention initiatives and maximize long-term business profitability.

---

## 06. Inventory Risk Analysis

### Business Question

Which products are at risk of stock shortages or overstock based on sales demand and inventory levels?

### Business Insight

Support inventory optimization by identifying products that require immediate replenishment or inventory reduction.

---

## 07. Supplier Performance Analysis

### Business Question

Which suppliers consistently provide the best purchasing value based on sales contribution and product performance?

### Business Insight

Evaluate supplier performance to strengthen procurement decisions and supplier partnerships.

---

## 08. Sales Trend & Growth Analysis

### Business Question

How has sales performance changed over time, and what are the key growth trends?

### Business Insight

Monitor business growth, identify seasonal patterns, and support strategic sales forecasting.

---

## 09. Product Profitability Analysis

### Business Question

Which products generate the highest profit, and which products reduce overall business profitability?

### Business Insight

Identify profitable products to improve pricing strategies, product portfolio decisions, and profit optimization.

---

## 10. Executive Business KPI Dashboard

### Business Question

What are the company's key business performance indicators from an executive management perspective?

### Business Insight

Provide executives with a high-level overview of revenue, profit, customer performance, product performance, and operational KPIs to support strategic decision-making.
